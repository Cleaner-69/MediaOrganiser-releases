# Media Organiser

Media Organiser tidies a folder of films and TV episodes into a neat library, with the names that Plex,
Jellyfin, Emby and Kodi understand straight away.

For example, `Avatar.2009.1080p.BluRay.x264.mkv` in your downloads becomes
`Movies\Avatar (2009)\Avatar (2009).mkv`, and `Breaking.Bad.S01E02.mkv` becomes
`Series\Breaking Bad\Season 01\Breaking Bad - S01E02.mkv`.

This page is where test versions are shared. It is a test version: thank you for trying it!

## Download

1. Click **Releases** on the right of this page (or [go straight to the newest version](../../releases/latest)).
2. Under **Assets**, click the file that ends in **.zip** to download it.
   (You can ignore the `.sig` file - the app uses it to check that the download is genuine.)
3. Unzip it into a folder of its own, for example `Documents\Media Organiser`.
4. Double-click **MediaOrganiser.exe**. Nothing needs to be installed.

If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
Windows shows this for new programs it does not know yet.

Keep MediaOrganiser.exe in the folder you unzipped it to. Its settings belong to that place.

## Your files are safe

- The app never deletes anything on its own. Spare copies and anything it is unsure about go to a
  **Holding** folder inside your library, and only you decide what happens to them there.
- **History & Undo** (top right in the app) can put back every file that was moved.
- Try it on a **copy** of some of your films first, not on your only copy.

## How to use it

1. **Folder to tidy**: the folder with your downloads or messy files.
2. **Your library folder**: where the tidy library should go (an empty folder is fine).
3. **Library style**: the app you watch with (Plex, Jellyfin, Emby or Kodi). It decides how files are named.
4. Follow the bar under the folders. It always shows the next step and the button to press:
   **Scan**, then **Confirm titles**, then **Pick copies**, then **Move files**.
5. Hover over any status in the list to see what will happen to that file.

## New versions

- Each new version is listed under **Releases**, newest at the top, with a short note of what changed.
- Updating is easy: download the new zip and unzip it into the **same folder**, replacing the old
  MediaOrganiser.exe. Your settings and choices stay.
- Older versions stay on the Releases page. If a new version gives you trouble, you can download the one
  before it and unzip that into the same folder.
- The version you have is shown at the top of the app window.

## Privacy

The app keeps its logs on your own PC. It does not send your files, your file names or your logs anywhere.
To look up film and series titles it asks The Movie Database (TMDb) online.
